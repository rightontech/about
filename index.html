Story

I got flown to Brisbane to pitch the CEO of a 43-store national trade supplier. Six months earlier I was working at their warehouse in Bunbury.

Find a problem that's real.
Then actually build the solution.

I started Right On Technologies after noticing something at Samios Plumbing Supplies. Staff were spending roughly two days every week manually matching customer order codes to supplier SKUs — copy, search, paste, repeat. I built the automation in Python + Claude API + Airtable. Then I got in front of the CEO and the board of BGW Group. We put a $14k pilot on the table with a $36k/store/year rollout model. The pipeline is still open.

While that plays out, I'm running a web design agency targeting hospitality businesses across Southeast Asia. Most small businesses have websites that look like they were built in 2014, and the owners know it. I build premium animated sites using GSAP and Vercel, using Claude Code to move faster than a traditional agency. The gap between what I charge and what an agency charges for the same quality is the whole pitch.
